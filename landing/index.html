Why NeoRouter

Stop juggling AI accounts.

🎁

Use what is free first

Many AI providers give away a lot for free every day. NeoRouter tracks those allowances and uses them before anything costs money. In free mode it never touches a paid model.

🛟

Always an answer

When a model is overloaded, out of quota or slow, the next good one takes over in a moment. You don't notice, and you don't have to retry.

🧠

Gets smarter with use

NeoRouter remembers which models answer quickly and reliably and picks them more often. Models that keep failing take a break.

💶

Spending under control

Give each key a budget, say $5 a month, and NeoRouter stops there. You always see what was used and what is left.

Privacy

Your conversations stay yours.

NeoRouter runs on your own server. It never saves what you write or what the AI answers. It only keeps simple counts such as how many requests went to which model.

  • Your provider keys are stored encrypted and are visible only to you.
  • Every person on your server has a private account.
  • Optional two-step sign-in with an authenticator app.
  • Open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence.
