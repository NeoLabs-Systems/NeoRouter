Ask for auto and the router decides. Nothing is hardcoded: every call teaches it which models are fast, reliable and worth their price.

01

Learns

Errors, timeouts, empty answers and slow responses earn penalty points and cooldowns. A circuit breaker pauses broken models, and good behaviour wins them back.

02

Spreads load

Bursts are distributed across the best candidates, by in-flight requests and remaining quota, instead of hammering one model until it hits its limit.

03

Respects money

Keys can be free-only (strictly 100 % free models) or carry a USD budget. The router stops at the limit and tracks every free-tier quota.

Setup

Paste a key. Get a catalog.

Over 80 providers are built in, and any OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible endpoint can be added. Keys are checked on the spot and stored encrypted. Models, prices and limits are imported automatically.

  1. Add providers: Groq, Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral, your local Ollama, …
  2. Create a router key with a mode, budget and routing profile.
  3. Point any OpenAI or Anthropic SDK at /v1.
  4. Use auto, auto:smart or provider/model.
Self-hosted first

Your keys never leave your server.

One Node.js process with SQLite. The public site is /, the dashboard is /app and the gateway is /v1. Only request metadata is logged, never prompts or answers.
